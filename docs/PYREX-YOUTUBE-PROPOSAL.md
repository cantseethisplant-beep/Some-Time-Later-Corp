# Proposal for Pyrex — Three-Part YouTube Series

**Project:** Some Time Later Corp / Eve Cloud  
**Project creator:** VelesPhylakos (not Exorock)  
**Status:** Working draft — not approved for publication

## Purpose

Propose three YouTube videos covering the introduction, development and eventual launch of an EVE Online corporation software platform.

The project has three main ambitions:

1. Make useful EVE corporation software easier and cheaper to deploy.
2. Encourage community participation, including developers, AI-assisted contributors and corporation members offering hardware.
3. Develop a sustainable platform that could eventually provide passive ISK income for the corporation.

## Video 1 — The Concept and Community Recruitment

Introduce the proposed application, how it is intended to work and how it will be developed.

Explain the public GitHub project and invite interested corporation members and viewers to contribute to the design, research, development and testing.

**Objective:** Build awareness and recruit contributors.

## Video 2 — Development Progress Through to Completion

Document the development journey, community contributions, significant milestones, problems encountered, testing and completed functionality.

Show actual results rather than presenting unverified capabilities as finished.

**Objective:** Demonstrate the project's progress and the contribution of the community.

## Video 3 — Finished Product, Lower Costs and Promotion

Present the completed application and demonstrate how corporations can use it.

Compare its actual costs with existing setup services, some of which have been advertised at 2 billion ISK or more.

Explain the potential savings, the benefits to smaller corporations and the longer-term ambition to generate passive corporation ISK.

**Objective:** Promote adoption of a completed, working product.

## Coordinated Launch — YouTube and EVE Forums

The third video should be created and edited once the application has reached a completed, tested and release-ready state.

It must **not be published immediately after editing**. Publication should be coordinated with the official EVE Online forum advertisement.

### Launch sequence

1. Complete the application and verify its advertised features.
2. Record, edit and finalise Pyrex's launch video.
3. Prepare the EVE Online forum advertisement, including the application description, availability, instructions, pricing if applicable, and relevant links.
4. Ensure the application, documentation and any required support arrangements are live and operational.
5. Agree a launch date and time with Pyrex.
6. Publish the YouTube video and EVE forum advertisement together, or as close together as practical, with the forum post live before viewers are directed to it.
7. Link the video, forum advertisement and public GitHub information so viewers can move easily between them.

**Critical requirement:** When viewers see the public launch video, they must be able to find the live EVE forum advertisement and access the completed application.

If the application, video or advertisement is not ready, postpone the coordinated launch rather than publish an incomplete promotion.

## Long-Term Goal — Passive Corporation ISK

One of the project's end goals is to turn the platform into a self-sustaining corporation asset capable of generating recurring ISK.

Potential revenue models remain subject to design, testing and verification against CCP's applicable rules.

The platform should aim to remain affordable, easy to operate and sustainable, with minimal routine administration.

## Attribution

The project should be credited to **VelesPhylakos**, not Exorock.

Community contributors should receive appropriate recognition.

## Proposed Role for Pyrex

Pyrex would help introduce the project to the EVE community, encourage participation, document development and present the final application through his YouTube channel.

For the final launch, Pyrex and the project team would coordinate publication of the completed video with the EVE Online forum advertisement.

This is a proposed collaboration, not an expectation that Pyrex personally funds or develops the application.
